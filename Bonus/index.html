French Bread by Jenn Hall

Recipe source: AllRecipes: French Bread

Image credit: Jenn Hall via AllRecipes.com

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon cornmeal
  • 6 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 ½ (.25 ounce) packages active dry yeast
  • 1 ½ teaspoons salt
  • 2 cups warm water (110°F)
  • 1 egg white
  • 1 tablespoon water

Equipment

  • Baking sheet
  • Mixing bowl
  • Knife
  • Brush
  • Parchment paper

Directions

  1. Grease a large baking sheet and sprinkle with cornmeal.
  2. Combine 2 cups flour, yeast, and salt in a bowl. Stir in 2 cups warm water; beat with dough hook.
  3. Add remaining flour gradually. Knead dough until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place dough in greased bowl, cover, and let rise for 1 hour.
  5. Punch down, divide in half, let rest 10 minutes.
  6. Roll out, form loaves, place seam-down on baking sheet.
  7. Brush with egg white + water. Let rise 35–40 minutes.
  8. Make diagonal cuts, bake at 375°F for 20 minutes.
  9. Brush again and bake 15–20 minutes more.
  10. Bread should sound hollow or register 190°F internally.
